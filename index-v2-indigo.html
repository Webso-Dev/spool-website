What we maintain

Systems an outside vendor built for you and still keeps running: order handling, production planning, the integrations around your ERP.

  • Version updatesFrameworks, runtimes and libraries, kept current.
  • Infrastructure updatesServers, databases and cloud services.
  • API maintenanceWhen a supplier or bank changes their interface.
  • Security fixesPatched as soon as a vulnerability is published.
  • Bug fixesReported by your team, or found in your error logs.
  • Small improvementsA new field, a report, a changed rule.

What we don’t do

New systems built from scratch, packaged software, the core of a standard ERP, or low-code configuration. If it’s outside this list, we’ll tell you before we start.

How it works

  1. We take over the handover

    We read your code, your current vendor’s tickets and your infrastructure, and write down what we find. Most handovers take two to three weeks.

  2. You set the limits

    Decide which kinds of change can go live on their own, and which need your approval first. You can tighten or loosen this at any time.

  3. Every ticket gets finished

    Our software plans, builds, tests and deploys most changes. Our engineers finish the rest. You see one queue and talk to one owner.
