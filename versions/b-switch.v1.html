Questions we get from IT managers

Will it work on code you didn’t write?+

That’s what the first week is for. We read your code and your vendor’s open tickets before we change anything, and we tell you honestly if something is outside what we can maintain.

Do we have to cancel our vendor first?+

No. Your vendor stays on during the switch. You end the contract when Spool has the whole queue and you’re confident in it.

Who answers when something breaks?+

Spool does. You have one queue and one owner. Our engineers review anything outside the limits you set, and finish every ticket software can’t.

What happens to our knowledge if we leave?+

It stays with you. Every change comes with a changelog entry and a decision record in your own repository, and we work in your cloud accounts.

How is it priced?+

On the amount of work we maintain, from €30,000 a year. You get a fixed estimate after we’ve looked at your systems.
